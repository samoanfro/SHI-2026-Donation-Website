01 / WHY SOCIAL HEALTH

Health has
three pillars.

Social health is how we build and care for our relationships: feeling known, finding belonging, and being there for one another.

David Kozlowski’s vision places social health alongside physical and mental health as a third, complementary pillar. SHI aims to turn that vision into education and opportunities to practice connection—in our schools, our communities, and our everyday lives.

◯

PHYSICAL HEALTH

How we care
for our bodies.

Movement, rest, and everyday habits that support our physical well-being.

≈

MENTAL HEALTH

How we care
for our minds.

Understanding our thoughts and emotions, and seeking support when we need it.

◎

SOCIAL HEALTH

How we care
for each other.

Building meaningful relationships, a sense of belonging, and the skills to connect.

THE HEART OF OUR MISSION

Connection takes practice: reaching out, listening, asking for support, and making room for someone else. Our prevention-focused approach starts with these everyday skills, rather than waiting until someone feels disconnected.

BEFORE YOU GIVE

A little more clarity.

Do I need to donate to take the Connection Challenge?

No. Reach out, make a meaningful connection, and invite three friends. Participation is free and giving is optional.

How will my donation be processed?

Once donations open, our button will take you to SHI’s official PayPal donation page. You’ll choose your amount and complete payment there. This website never asks for your card details or PayPal password.

Will Venmo and monthly giving be available?

Venmo and recurring giving appear in our PayPal setup preview. We’ll confirm the options on the final hosted page before opening donations. Available payment methods and recurring choices will be shown at checkout.

Where will the money go?

The proposed allocation covers education, school and community programs, platform development, outreach, and administration. The $250,000 goal and allocation are pending board approval. View the full proposed budget.

Are donations tax-deductible?

SHI’s final nonprofit and donation disclosures are awaiting verification. We are not making a tax-deductibility claim in this preview.
